A Century of Love, Baked Daily.

Since 1924, Gracy's has been more than a bakery; it's a testament to the enduring power of family traditions. Our recipes have been passed down through generations, each loaf a tangible connection to our heritage. We believe that true luxury lies in time-honored craftsmanship and the simple, profound joy of breaking bread together.

"The secret ingredient is always time."

— Eleanor Gracy, Founder

Uncompromising Quality.

Our commitment to excellence begins long before the oven is heated. We source 100% of our organic ingredients from local, sustainable farms that share our dedication to the earth. Every grain of wheat, every drop of milk, is chosen with uncompromising standards.

We eschew modern shortcuts in favor of traditional, slow-fermentation methods. This painstaking process, often taking over 48 hours for a single loaf, develops complex flavor profiles and textures that cannot be rushed. It is the artisanal way, the Gracy's way.
