Our Heritage

Founded in 1982, Gracy's began as a modest storefront dedicated to the slow, deliberate art of natural leavening. Today, we continue that tradition with unwavering commitment.

The Craft

We believe that true flavor cannot be rushed. Our mother dough, carefully nurtured for over four decades, provides the complex, earthy foundation for every loaf we bake. We source our grains exclusively from small, independent mills that prioritize soil health over yield.

The Community

A bakery is more than a place of commerce; it is a vital organ of the neighborhood. We strive to foster a sense of gathering, offering not just sustenance, but a moment of quiet connection in an otherwise hurried world.
